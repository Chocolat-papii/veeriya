GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How do I get your delivery address? +

Contact vee.RIYA on WhatsApp before checking out. We’ll provide the correct address and any instructions you need.

Can I shop from any online store? +

Many stores may work, but it depends on the retailer’s checkout and delivery rules. Ask us to confirm before placing your order.

How long does delivery take? +

Delivery times can vary based on the store, parcel arrival, shipping schedule and customs processing. Contact us for the latest estimate.

Are there extra charges? +

Please confirm whether customs duties, local delivery, special handling or other charges apply before paying for shipping.
